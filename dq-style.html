What this deck claims

  • One measure. Delay and loss in a single distribution, for a path, in each direction.
  • It composes. The path's measure is the sum of its parts, so a requirement at the top becomes a budget at every hop.
  • It is cheap to get. A low-rate test stream, a few thousand samples, timestamps at two or more points.
  • It tells you which lever. What the design did, what the link rate did, what contention did.
Chapter 2 defines the measure. Chapter 3 shows how to get it. Chapter 4 shows how to contract and budget with it.
