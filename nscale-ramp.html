1 · Studies

Cloud / DC fabric

Cloud Native Data Center Networking — Dinesh Dutt IN PROGRESS Linux-NOS worldview, automation-first — the closest match to the SR Linux operating model. Nothing on RoCE or rail (~2019).
Deploying Juniper Data Centers with EVPN VXLAN — Aninda Chatterjee
Mastering Advanced Juniper Data Centers — Chatterjee Advanced volume: multicast, inter-VRF, Apstra, plus six interoperability chapters.
· Interop is the most transferable part of a Juniper book for a Nokia-bound reader — interop is the only place an author must describe the protocol rather than the product.
· Inter-VRF is directly the job: multi-tenant cloud, controlled leaking to shared services and egress.
· Multicast — the BUM-replication half (underlay multicast VNI vs ingress replication, serialisation skew).
· Apstra — concepts (intent, blueprint, generated config, drift), not the product; they're a Nokia shop.

2 · Hands-on — the Nscale replica lab

SR Linux throughout, DC and WAN. Containerlab, on EC2 i-0e4680c8f360675ab (eu-west-2). The laptop is only a terminal.

Why SR Linux in the WAN too: it does SR-MPLS broadly, and SRv6 on the 7730 SXR — Nokia's WAN-capable SR Linux platform. A greenfield AI-cloud WAN needs no BNG or subscriber management, so there's no obvious reason to reach for SR-OS.

Topology — asymmetric, ~15 devices + 8 hosts

LayerNodes
Region A front-end2 spines, 3 leaves — EVPN/VXLAN, symmetric IRB, tenant VRFs, storage VNI
Region A back-end2 rails + 1 back-end spine — rail-optimised, 1:1, RoCEv2 lossless
SR core3 nodes, TI-LFA; both SR-MPLS and SRv6, so the comparison is measured rather than asserted
Region B (thin)1 spine, 1 leaf, 1 border
Bordersbl-a, bl-b
Hosts2 tenants, 3 GPU nodes dual-homed to both fabrics, 1 storage, 2 in region B
AWSreal VPC + Transit Gateway + Site-to-Site VPN into bl-a — client onboarding, eBGP over tunnel, IPsec MTU

Region B is deliberately thin: it exists to make the backbone and inter-region transfer real, not to duplicate a GPU fabric.

The enabler: tc rate-limits on the links. veth pairs forward at memory speed, so nothing congests and ECN never marks. Without this, half of S5 is theatre.
